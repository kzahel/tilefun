Existing standing approvals do not transfer to a new seated mesh.

[retained identity inventory](../art-source/vehicle-riders/appearance-inventory-v1.json) pins the actual PNGs, source records, current picker/promoted snapshot/EntityDefs, every native frame bound and full palette. Classic frame is16x16; legacy people16x32 with6frames per direction; promoted32x32 with4.

The20 legacy humans share a sprite format but retain different faces/skin tones/hair or hats/clothes/pack shapes. The other5 promoted characters require animal anatomy, muzzles, ears, paws and tails. Proportions and contact policy: preserve native head/torso/limb/hat/ear/tail ratios measured against a shared world-pixel ruler. Do not individually auto-fit every appearance to the same height or width. Classic visible height16 is about0.64 of a25px promoted front silhouette; legacy standing spans range22-26px by pose, hats included. Those measurements guide distinct anatomy, never a per-angle scale correction. Existing EntityDefs list Classic physicalHeight12, legacy24, and promoted24; Bear width14 vs10 for the others. These are separate standing/catalog metadata, not measurements of seated3D anatomy. PlayerModels.applyPlayerModel explicitly preserves the actual player collider when changing appearance; this proposal does not alter authority/physics.

The immutable upright-v1 profile remains Explorer-only evidence. For another body, first measure its own hip bottom, limb lengths, joint ranges and actual contact surfaces. Attempt profile reach without changing its proportions. If unreachable, retain the identity and propose a separately reviewed contact-profile/bike fit variant; do not stretch arms/legs or silently translate targets to manufacture a pass. Classic short paws, Bear bulk, Dog muzzle/ears and Squirrel tail are especially likely to expose distinct reach/clearance limits. No universal fit is asserted.

Suggested bounded order, only after parent checks the first pose: (1) one extreme proportion pilot, preferably Brown Bear or Classic as parent chooses; (2) one actual bike with measured saddle/grips/pegs/body/wheel envelope and an explicit mount; (3) a small reviewed human-style pilot with a cap/long-hair/garment extreme before any20-person batch; (4) separate feline/dog/squirrel templates and individual identity checks. The20 human appearances may share construction helpers, but require measured per-identity head/hair/skin/costume/garment geometry and paint, not generic Explorer substitution.

Each later appearance/profile/bike combination would need packed editable semantic components, its own identity/UV palette evidence, actual contacts and displaced/reset negatives, self-intersection and straddle diagnostics, head/torso/tail/body/wheel clearance, native32combined/alone views and depth occlusion, plus exact source replay. Authority/prediction, real parented identity, two riders, boarding/exit/reset/reuse, overhead bounds, traffic occupancy and occupied fallback remain separate engine gates owned by the parent. This proposal does not authorize that rollout or claim all-avatar/AI/fallback support.

Explorer source prototype is complete; these remaining identities are not production coverage. See [rider prerequisites](topics/vehicle-riders.md).
