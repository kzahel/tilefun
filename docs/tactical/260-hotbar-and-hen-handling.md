# 260 Hotbar and hen handling

Add four saved item slots and a selected stack. Convert the initial corn and
wood baskets on load. Replicate the item in the avatar's hand while keeping
the slot contents on the owning seat's stream.

Holding corn leads Buff hens. Scatter some on clear ground near the flock.
Use the generated farm gate or place your own in Build. The authority checks
the gate opening before closing it. Hens moved out of their home can settle
near the place where their player puts away the corn.

The [farming topic](../topics/farming.md) owns the Play controls and save
contract. Roosters, chicks and town stalls build on these mechanics in a later
slice.
