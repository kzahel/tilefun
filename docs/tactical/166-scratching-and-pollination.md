## Contract

Chickens approach finite soil patches, alternate real backward foot scratches,
uncover one actual seed, lower the head, pick it up and eat. Saved stock/timers,
claims, interruption and attachment conserve all portions. Empty soil persists.

Bees fly between finite nectar flowers. One real sip takes one portion. Pollen
from a distinct flower marks the destination and advances at most one nearby
admitted depleted crop timer by at most 30 seconds, never below one second.
Each destination accepts at most one transfer per 60 ready simulation seconds.
Full sources discard surplus time; no offline growth or manufactured loose food.

Hummingbirds approach actual bloom contact points, hover with tucked feet and
beating connected wings, sip finite nectar and briefly guard that flower against
nearby unprotected conspecifics. Warnings use normal alarm/escape authority and
never injure actors. Scares, followers, rides, source deletion and unready scopes
retain ordinary protections. Guarding is bounded, then releases the flower.

Sources are ordinary editor actors. Fresh natural homes receive
bounded cell-owned supplies without moving original animals or remapping RNG.
Manual placement creates no resources or neighbours. Worker, saves, replication
and Canvas/GPU use one production behavior implementation; no private lab AI.
