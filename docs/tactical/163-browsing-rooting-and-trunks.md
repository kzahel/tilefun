# Browsing, rooting and trunks

Owner authorized the proposed next three on 2026-10-20: goat shrub browsing and
rock hopping, pig rooting, and elephant trunk harvesting/shaking fruit. Implement
end to end, add ordinary-authority lab demonstrations, validate, commit and push.
This is a bounded action-animation exception for these existing animals; no old
bank changes, new species, inferred art approval or unrelated repair work.

## Contract

Goats approach finite leafy shrubs, rear above planted hind hooves to browse,
consume one portion, lower and occasionally hop onto an actual low rock to rest
before hopping down. Takeoff, flight, landing and edited/removed support must
respect actual geometry, collision, heights, residency and player protections.

Pigs sniff a real root patch, lower the snout and root visibly, uncover one actual
food item, then eat it. Root patches contain bounded stock and replenish slowly
only while admitted and ready; disturbed soil shows depletion. No unlimited food
or cache refill. Deletion and interruption conserve anything already uncovered.

Elephants approach real fruit trees from a body-safe position, reach with the
trunk and take one portion; after repeated harvests they can shake available fruit
loose for other foragers. Fruit transfer conserves the shared finite tree stock.
The trunk visibly reaches, curls the held fruit to the mouth, eats and releases.
Tree removal or unavailable support cancels/freezes safely; no private lab AI.

All actions use shared Realm/Worker authority, ordinary replicated poses and saved
work/resource components. Respect readiness/planning budgets, natural-only delayed
meal returns, scare/follow/ride protections and no offline catch-up. Natural homes
receive bounded supplies with stable cell-owned IDs; manually placed animals do
not manufacture resources. Editor resources make the behavior available in saves.

Retain editable Blender sources, fixed mesh volumes and connected fixed-length
limbs/trunk segments. Inspect current native travel first and append action cells
without changing old pixels, scale or camera. New review identities stay pending.
Record plans and independent evidence in the canonical behavior ledger; refresh
the production table at checkpoints. Three Animal Browser activity links exercise
normal production authority.
