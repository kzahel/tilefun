# 259 Town farming

Deliver a short Play loop at generated farmsteads. Name farms after their
nearby settlement and give the regional Map an offscreen farm destination.
Use the existing regional road and terrain constraints for outskirts farms.

Four corn beds, a chicken pen and two small trees sit together at the arrival.
Keyboard, controller and touch share contextual actions. The player carries corn
and wood in a small basket. Progress uses the existing shared world authority
and per-player save. Build mode can place the garden components freely.

See [Farming](../topics/farming.md) for the controls, persistence contract,
and Workshop garden layout.
