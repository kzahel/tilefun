# 269 Held-item actions

Status: delivered · 2026-11-22. Owner request: the character's main actions
should come from what they hold. Throwing a ball becomes one hotbar item with
infinite balls, plus a stick to swing and an axe to chop trees, behind a use
button separate from Interact, and a quick item switch in the spirit of a
"pick from a strip" selector.
Owner: [Steam Deck and menus](../topics/steam-deck-and-menus.md) owns the button
scheme; [farming](../topics/farming.md) owns the hotbar save contract and chopping.

## Decisions (owner-approved brainstorm)

- **X uses what is in your hand; Y uses what is in front of you.** The old Throw
  action is now **Use** (`use_item`): X, Q/B (left keys), `.` (right keys) and the
  touch paddle, whose label names the verb (Throw, Swing, Chop, Scatter, Wave).
  Y/E stays Interact; B stays vehicle equipment.
- **One hotbar, tools never run out.** Eight slots. Ball, stick and axe are tools
  with no count that every player carries (creative-first, nothing to earn).
  Countable stacks (corn, wood) keep their counts.
- **Empty hands wave**, so the use button is never dead.
- **No pause to choose.** Tap D-pad ←/→ (or `[`/`]`, `-`/`=`) to step; hold to keep
  stepping. A picker strip above the hotbar names the item and its verb while
  choosing. D-pad ↑ (C, `\`) swaps back to the last item. Co-op is never paused.
- **Stick and axe are harmless to animals.** A swing startles wildlife and scares
  wandering NPCs exactly as a ball hit does, and pops angry ghosts for a gem.
- **Axe scope:** woodlot trees only. Chopping generated world trees would need
  persisted edits to generated terrain and stays out of scope.

## What changed

- **Inventory** (`inventory/Inventory.ts`): `BALL`, `STICK`, `AXE` items with
  `tool`/`verb` metadata, `HOTBAR_SIZE` 8, `withStarterTools`, a `previous`
  selection for `{ swap: true }`. Four-slot saves pad out; a player who gains the
  tools with empty hands holds the ball, so X keeps throwing for existing players.
  `changeStack` refuses tools.
- **One replicated hand byte** (`handItem`/`countHandUse` in `inventory/Inventory.ts`): the entity field `heldItem`
  became `hand`, packing the item (5 bits) with a 3-bit use counter. Viewers start
  a use pose when the counter changes. This avoids a separate event channel and
  the last free bit of the u32 entity delta mask.
- **Authority** (`server/ItemUse.ts`): one `use-item` message (`dirX`, `dirY`,
  `force`; replaces `throw-ball`) resolved from the held item. Ball: the existing
  owned-ball throw. Stick: swing disc ahead (12 px ahead, 14 px radius) bats loose
  balls with charge-scaled speed (re-arming fetch for owned toys) and applies the
  ball-impact reaction to other bodies. Axe: the same reaction plus a `chop` on
  the woodlot tree ahead through `performFarmAction`. Corn: `feed` on the nearest
  hen (no hens, no use). Empty: wave. 250 ms cooldown except for throws.
  `performFarmAction` now requires the axe in hand to chop.
- **Prompts:** chop and scatter prompts name the Use button; without the axe a
  woodlot tree says "Hold the 🪓 axe to chop this tree". Interact and a tap on the
  prompt still perform them.
- **Presentation:** `public/assets/farming/held-items.png` grows to 6×3 cells
  (generated by `scripts/gen-held-items.mjs`; corn and wood pixels unchanged):
  ball, stick, axe and a cartoon glove, with wind-up and follow-through rows for
  the stick and axe. `rendering/HandPose.ts` poses the attachment by entity id on
  one internal clock, shared by the scene sprite pass and the first-person HUD.
- **Labs:** the scenario `throw-ball` instrument puts the ball in hand, then sends
  `use-item`; recipe worlds accept `use-item` from their seat.

## Validation

- Unit: `server/ItemUse.test.ts` (default ball throw and counter, stick batting a
  ball and popping a ghost, cooldown, axe chop, wave, corn without hens),
  `server/FarmPlay.test.ts` (migration, padding, swap, tools never spent, chop
  needs the axe), `rendering/HandPose.test.ts`, and the updated input, scenario,
  lab and wildlife suites. `npm run typecheck` and Biome on changed files pass.
- Browser (production build): farming, split-screen hotbar, dog fetch and the
  frog/robin/wildlife throw cases pass.
- Headless captures of the real client confirmed the carried tools, the stick
  and axe wind-up/sweep and the glove wave. A glove lifted 11 px above the hand
  was not drawn by the scene; attachments stay within a few pixels of the hand.
