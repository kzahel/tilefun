## Contract

Fruit/nut trees and growing plants replenish bounded visible stock over active
simulation time (180–300 seconds per portion), preserving saved timers. Loose food
and caches never manufacture food. Theft transfers one actual cache item; the
squirrel can restock from real supplies. Tree deletion cancels support and growth.

Thrown toys retain ownership and original creation order across fetch/rethrow and
save/reload. Ten per player per realm, counting unloaded actors; evict the oldest
loose ball. Carried toys are protected; reject an extra throw if all ten are held.
No ordinary expiry; owned toys remain on the water surface too. Editor balls
retain their legacy lifetime and water loss.

Use shared Worker/Realm authority, existing readiness/planning budgets, ordinary
replicated poses and attachments. Preserve friendly-animal protections and delayed
natural-only meal replacements. Retain new editable sources and exact review banks;
