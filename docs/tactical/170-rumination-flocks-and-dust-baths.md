- Cow takes a finite grass portion, lowers into a grounded resting posture,
  chews that saved meal, then rises. Interrupted rumination retains the paid meal;
  chewing cannot create food or count an unpaid meal.

  flock with normal collision and heading, and visibly bleat when reunited.
  Group identity, companion protections and bounded home/planning limits apply.
- Donkey seeks an ordinary saved dust patch, lowers, rolls with connected limbs,
  then rises. A completed bath leaves persistent scuffs on the patch and records
  the bath. Moving/deleting/raising a patch interrupts work without remote effects.
