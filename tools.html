Shared approvals & reports

Building Lab →

Review apartment, hotel, and storefront buildings plus complete block arrangements. The dense neighborhood run reviews three views of the same playable two-by-two district, with apartments, shops, a hotel, connected streets, and a pocket green. Its explorer link lets you create and play there. The road foundation run reviews nine surface scenes from the annotated giant sheet. The street starter run has six focused furniture scenes using shared gameplay props, with its own review queue. Previous/Next moves through the queue; Looks right approves and hides a candidate. Needs changes saves your reason. Two reports pause the batch until you say “ready” in chat.
