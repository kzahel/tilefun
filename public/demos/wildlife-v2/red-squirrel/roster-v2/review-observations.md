# Red squirrel observations

Agent inspection: russet coat, tufted ears, cream muzzle/paws and a broad curled
tail read as a squirrel at native size and 3x. Tail remains a connected furry
ribbon rather than separate rounded beads. Compression and tucked paws support
the shared physical bound. Feeding lifts the chest and forepaws; a dark nut
between pale hands is clearest in front/profile, naturally hidden at the rear.
The small native feeding detail is subtle. All four facings and chronological
action contacts were inspected. Independent source, replay and Chromium
evidence remain separate; these observations do not invent human approval.
