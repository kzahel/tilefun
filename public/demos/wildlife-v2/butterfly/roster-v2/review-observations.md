# Butterfly observations

Agent inspection: orange cells, dark wing borders and cream marginal spots
remain visible at tiny native scale. Front/rear show bilateral wings; profile
uses real foreshortening and folded/open silhouettes. Basking rest preserves
recognition, and the first flutter pose joins it. Four fixed panels beat
independently while legs tuck. Proboscis and resting-wing flex provide the
action; physical airborne arcs and ground landings belong to shared authority.
Native and enlarged contacts/playback were inspected. Source, replay and
exact-crop evidence are separate; these observations are not human approval.
