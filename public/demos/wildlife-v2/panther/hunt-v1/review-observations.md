# Panther hunt-v1 observations

Review status: pending human review. Coordinator inspection is technical/art
triage, not an owner approval. Explicit scope: tactical 132 only.

A lean unstriped melanistic big cat: narrower torso, shoulders and pelvis; smaller cheek/skull volumes and ears; an eight-segment curved tail. The charcoal/slate palette keeps the back, belly and near/far legs readable; amber eye pixels survive the front and side views.

The fixed 40-degree wildlife camera, 10 pixels/unit and ground anchor [48,72]
match the Explorer/elephant comparison. Source and final native chronological
walk, lowered-head sniff and jaw display have been inspected in all four
facings at 1x and enlarged integer scale. Shoulder/pelvis pitch and torso rise
remain visible while the skull stays a fixed volume. The saved Blender audit
checks 1,392 fixed-length links, 432 planted contacts and stance travel
compensation. Paws meet the floor; no whole-image bounce substitutes for a gait.

The actual full-Chromium playback visits all 29 chronological states at native
and 4x size with no page/resource errors. Every exported crop matches the player
in every facing (232 checks across both scales). The sequence includes two
walk cycles, sniff, display and idle, plus a separate travelling comparison.
Fresh Blender/finish replay reproduces nine public raster/metadata artifacts
and the palette-letter masters byte for byte; Blender container bytes are not
claimed deterministic. Camera, editable geometry, paint masters, scripts,
playback and final exports are pinned in the receipt.

The dark profile is deliberately compact. Rear face detail stays quiet and far paws merge briefly at crossings. At native scale this reads as a stylized panther, without a claim of zoological photorealism.

Runtime animation remains provisional: stalk/patrol use this planted walk;
chase accelerates the same stride, the short grounded catch uses jaw display,
and feeding uses the lowered-head sniff. No airborne attack, carcass or gore.
The authority behavior protects players, pets and farm animals, and replaces
only successfully eaten deer/rabbits with a new durable individual nearby.
