# Small dragon fierce-v1 — primary-agent observations

Pending human review. Inspected native four-facing scenery, chronological
enlarged display and crouched-rest strips, and the retained editable source audit.
The green quadruped has golden horns, ten-color broad body shading, purple/gold
bat-wing membranes, a hinged toothy jaw and a long tail. Folded wings make it
taller than its grounded body; native idle heights are 54px front, 51px rear and
56px in profile. Explorer and the elephant remain at their original pixel density.
The coin/chest/gem accessory is visible beside the creature in the review scenery.

The display opens the wings and jaw and emits three separately keyed gray puffs.
Puffs read clearly in the rear/profile views and can be occluded in the front;
they are deliberately small native marks rather than large screen effects.
Sleep crouches the body/head, folds the wings backward and brings the fixed-link
tail around its rear. The walk moves chest and pelvis with four distinct planted
footfall phases. The source cancels travel at grounded contacts while the body
rises and pitches; no full-frame bounce substitutes for physical limb motion.

Saved-scene inspection passes 196 poses, 2,352 fixed-link checks, 608 planted
contacts and 272 stance/travel compensation checks. Body walk motion remains
1.72–2.14 native pixels across the four views, with independent head/chest/pelvis
movement. Smoke visibility is checked against the saved source, and binary-alpha
body frames leave padding throughout the full bank. The static hoard is an
integer-vector accessory recipe, reproducible separately from the body audit.

Limitations: stylized rigid pieces and angular wing membranes are intentional;
this is not an anatomical study or a runtime skeletal model. Sleep is a crouched
rest with a curled tail, rather than a fully flattened sleeping silhouette.
Some far-side limbs/wing fingers overlap at the fixed camera. The flight artwork
is retained, while this release guards and hunts on the ground. Gameplay uses a
short original synthesized warning; players are never prey. Human approval is
separate from these agent observations and integrity checks.
