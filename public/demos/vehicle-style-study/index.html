Existing vehicle sprites

Original pixels, shown at the same zoom. New cars use a common camera and world scale; the original sprites have their own illustrated proportions.

What to look for

At native size, compare the silhouette, glass borders, wheel openings and red highlights. Scrub the diagonals to see which details stay readable. Switch to the raw render to see which choices come from geometry and which appear during pixel finishing.

These are workflow trials, so body design also affects the result. Producing good sprite inputs does not yet prove those pixels will make a good texture on a simpler runtime model.
