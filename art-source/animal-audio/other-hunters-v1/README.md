# Other hunter recordings v1

Six identified animal recordings supply eight excerpts: grey wolf, coyote, red
fox, Siberian lynx, spotted hyena and cheetah. Wolf/coyote/fox/lynx source pages
license their recordings under CC0. David Watts / British Library hyena is
CC BY 4.0, with the Wikimedia rights confirmation preserved by reference.
Smirnova, Volodin, Demina and Volodina (2016) cheetah calls are CC BY 4.0 from
PLOS ONE supplementary audio, distributed by Wikimedia Commons.

Freesound sources are public HQ MP3 previews rather than lossless downloads.
Commons files retain their original distributed Ogg bytes. Exact creators,
source/license/download links, hashes, byte counts and contextual qualifications
are in the provenance files and manifest. The [public credits](../../../public/assets/audio/animals/CREDITS.txt)
identify creators, licenses and changes and are linked from Animal Browser.

Wolf has one complete 7.7-second distant howl, with natural reverberation; coyote
has one 3.7-second howl. These use existing fresh display onsets only, with 45/35
second cooldowns and no idle ambience. Hyena whoops and lynx warning huffs also
use displays only. Fox barks sparsely at stationary idle, staying quiet during
its actual listening/stalking/pounce/eat actions. Cheetah has two real contact
meows, selected by comparison with the study's Figure 1 spectrogram; these can
play at stationary idle or fresh display. It does not receive a lion/tiger roar.
No generic placeholder is layered beneath a recorded voice. Dire wolf retains its
fantasy synthesis; it does not silently inherit the real grey-wolf recording.

Waveforms, spectra and source descriptions were inspected. Existing sound paths
and decoded signal limits are tested; **owner listening review remains pending**.
The source recordings retain their field/archival recording character. Processing
uses species-specific filters, mono 24 kHz, gain target RMS 0.16 limited by source
peak 0.75, quiet edges and Vorbis quality 4. Recipe and exact decoded output metrics
are retained. The explicit single-bank exporter now accepts up to 12 seconds for
complete natural howls and still refuses to overwrite frozen output banks.
Builds never fetch or regenerate recordings. See [campaign delivery](../../../docs/tactical/185-overnight-animal-voices.md).
