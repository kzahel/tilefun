# Further distinctive bird recordings v1

Six sources/ten excerpts cover ostrich, emu, cassowary, toucan, great blue heron
and Atlantic puffin. Exact sources, licenses, hashes, selected ranges, processing
and decoded measurements are retained. Public credits identify creator, source,
edits and license without implying endorsement. Freesound sources are public HQ
MP3 previews, not original lossless files; puffin retains the distributed Ogg.

Ostrich is an explicit non-vocal male nasal snort. Emu/cassowary use low
rumbles/grunts. The generic toucan uses the uploader's identified keel-billed
toucan croaks. Heron takes lie within the uploader's identified call window
(-48 to -44 seconds before the end, about 33.17–37.17 seconds from the start),
avoiding larger sharp transients. Nearby pond/geese/blackbird background remains
a listening-review concern; it is not presented as an isolated studio sound.

Puffin uses Lawrence Shove's 1965 Skokholm recording, copyright The British
Library Board, shelfmark W1CDR0001416 BD3. **The retained source and both edited
audio excerpts are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.** Their license and URL are
explicit on every derived-file receipt and in the public credits, alongside
the exact excerpt paths, creator, title, source and description of the edits.
Commons records permission ticket 2015111010010706.

All six voices use sparse stationary playback. Toucan/puffin stop while carrying;
none adds calls over feeding, flight or unrelated work. No new AI or animations.
Species filters, mono 24 kHz, RMS target 0.16 limited by source peak 0.75, quiet
edges and Vorbis quality 4. Frozen assets are consumed without re-encoding.
Source identity, waveforms/spectra and decoded signal limits were checked;
**owner listening review remains pending**, especially field/archival noise.
