# Quiet neighbor recordings v1

Six sources/nine excerpts cover mule, rat, chinchilla, red squirrel, gecko and
kinkajou. Exact public HQ MP3 previews, source/license links, creator credits,
hashes, ranges, processing and decoded metrics are retained. These are not
described as original lossless uploads. Public credits identify edits without
implying creator endorsement.

Rat/chinchilla/squirrel are restricted to fresh existing flee warnings; calm
idle, feeding and cache work stay quiet. Rat's source identifies a pet rat but
does not specify behavior. Chinchilla's entire source is about 0.117 seconds,
retained as one short sound without artificial variants. Squirrel's low recording
gain and field background remain a listening-review concern; it also stops while
carrying. The existing native alarm-only case checks all three actual serialized
flee onsets, calm silence, source selection, deduplication and reset silence.

Mule uses its own neigh/short bray rather than a horse or donkey substitution.
Generic gecko is explicitly interpreted as tokay gecko for sound. **Neil Francis
Tribucio recorded it**, with kevp888 uploading and applying light noise removal
and fades. Both are credited. Kinkajou uses an explicitly labeled young animal's
chirp; its uploader already edited out human speech. It adds no age/baby AI.
Mule/gecko/kinkajou calls are sparse stationary ambience, quiet through unrelated
work. No new animations or AI are implied by a sound route.

Species filters, mono 24 kHz, RMS target 0.16 limited by source peak 0.75, quiet
edges and Vorbis quality 4. Source identity, waveforms and decoded signal limits
were checked; **owner listening review remains pending**, particularly the very
short calls and squirrel background. Frozen audio-only exports are consumed
without downloading/re-encoding during builds.
