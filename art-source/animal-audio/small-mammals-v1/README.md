# Smaller mammal recordings v1

Six sources/ten excerpts cover guinea pig, meerkat, raccoon, North American river
otter, alpaca and llama. Exact source bytes, creator/download/license links,
hashes, trims, processing and decoded measurements are retained. Public credits
are linked from Animal Browser and identify edits without implying endorsement.

Guinea pig and alpaca use sparse stationary squeaks/soft calls. Alpaca excludes
larger noisy throat-scratching interruptions. Raccoon defensive growls/hisses play
only on fresh flee onsets; cache raids, calm rest and feeding remain quiet.
Meerkat adult recruitment calls come from the natural playback track in Johnson-
Ulrich et al. supplementary File S1, not their distorted parametric-speaker
rerecording. They play only on the existing fresh threat/alarm pose. This gives a
warning context without inventing new recruitment or mobbing AI.

Llama has a 0.24-second defensive air burst recorded while spitting at a goat.
This is explicitly a non-vocal sound, with fresh flee-onset playback and no calm
hum, spit projectile or new behavior. These warning-only profiles disable idle
ambience. Native coverage checks all fresh serialized alarm poses, calm silence,
correct recordings, deduplication and reset silence alongside the existing piglet.

River otter uses NPS-authored Canyonlands chirps from the sound gallery, with
water/field background filtered around the high calls. It plays sparingly at
stationary idle; this introduces no underwater listener or whale/dolphin playback.
No claim to original U.S. Government works. Source media is the original NPS MP3;
meerkat retains the original supplementary WAV; Freesound files are exact public
HQ MP3 previews rather than original lossless uploads.

Species filters, mono 24 kHz, RMS target 0.16 limited by source peak 0.75, quiet
edges and Vorbis quality 4. No artificial pitch variants or other-species
substitutions. Source identity/descriptions, waveforms/spectra and output signal
limits were checked; **owner listening review remains pending**, including field
noise and the very short llama burst. Frozen outputs are consumed by builds
without downloading/re-encoding. Sources:
