# Further mammal recordings v1

Five sources/six excerpts cover panda, koala, both giraffe forms, water buffalo
and Tasmanian devil. Source/export bytes, licenses, hashes, selected ranges,
processing and decoded measurements are retained. Public credits identify the
creators, source, edits and license without implying endorsement.

Panda uses the real male honk in Charlton et al. 2019 S6 Audio. Koala uses two
inhalation/exhalation phrases from Andy's bellow at Lone Pine Koala Sanctuary,
Charlton et al. 2011 Movie S1, not two complete bellows. The publisher's original
notice says CC Attribution; the linked Commons Open Access Media Importer copy
explicitly identifies CC BY 2.5, retained here. All authors are credited. The
original MOV container is retained, but shipped OGG files contain audio only.

Giraffe uses Baotic, Sicks and Stoeger's 2015 Additional file 5, Fig. 3a hum,
from the official PMC MP3 stream, not original lossless media. Their nocturnal
zoo recording establishes humming; its function and the caller's sleep state
remain unsettled. Both giraffe forms share this explicitly inherited voice.
Sparse local stationary playback adds no circadian, mating or communication AI.

Water buffalo is a short, explicitly non-vocal domestic Asian water-buffalo
nasal snort. Tasmanian devil uses digifishmusic's natural cry from the conservation
park only at fresh existing displays, with no idle/feeding cries or bear-synthesis
layer. Freesound media are exact public HQ MP3 previews, not lossless originals.

Species filters, mono 24 kHz, RMS target 0.16 limited by source peak 0.75, quiet
edges and Vorbis quality 4. The exporter excludes video/subtitle/data streams and
checks the exported stream inventory. Source identity, waveforms/spectra and
decoded signal limits were inspected; **owner listening review remains pending**.
Frozen assets are consumed without downloading or re-encoding during builds.
