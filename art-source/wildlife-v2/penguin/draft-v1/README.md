# Emperor penguin draft-v1

Fresh Aptenodytes forsteri adult geometry and palette-letter pixel masters. Production-agent draft judgment; coordinator/human review pending. No gameplay promotion.

Fixed wildlife-v2 orthographic camera: 40 degrees ABOVE GROUND, 10 world pixels/unit; yaws down180/up0/left100/right260. Canvas64 square, anchor32,44, ortho6.4, shift12/64. Standing profile22px (proposal26); down21/up22. Different swimming bounds are genuine rigid-body foreshortening, never resampling. Actual Modern Exteriors roof/tree/four-direction cars and approved Explorer are retained in scene comparisons. Water uses committed water tiles as a decorative backdrop, without refraction.

30 retained meshes,100 poses: idle1, waddle8, swim8, flipper stretch8 in four facings. Torso/skull and markings have fixed volume. Body waddle X=-.24*sin(2*pi*phase), Z unchanged; planted feet inverse-compensate that translation. Left landing0/right4, duty.75, travel preview.32units/cycle. Computed floor support2,2,2,1,2,2,2,1 counts exact liftoff boundaries; airborne apices right3/left7. Torso center lies inside the actual single-support web polygon at those apices. This geometric placement is not a mass/force simulation. Femur.22/tibia.30, legs largely concealed by coat; web bottoms are actual floor contacts. Rear heels omit front toe highlights.

Swimming rigidly pitches torso75 degrees and counter-pitches skull, with bodyZ1.35. Three fixed flipper links.44/.38/.22 per side use bent/twisted actual mesh articulation; tips stay behind shoulders, with both stroke halves and feathered recovery. All underwater mesh bounds.92955..2.16849 remain below waterZ3.0; no floor footfalls. Eight-pose stretch raises flippers and sweeps slightly backward so profile views retain readable movement, returning exactly to idle. Preview translation is separate from root-in-place sprites.

Build in an isolated factory-startup Blender5.2.2 process with --python-exit-code1. Run build.py, author-masters.py, finish.py. Fresh-load penguin.blend then run audit-source.py; audit-output.py reruns finishing and compares decoded images and actual browser pixels. capture.mjs uses only bundled full Chromium and closes its owned server/browser; review-panels.py builds chronological inspection panels. Mesh hashes and unit scales are audited before/after100 poses. Compact guides contain independently projected geometry/hulls/landmarks/contacts, not repeated full vertices. Full source geometry remains in penguin.blend.

Deliberate stylization: stable rounded black/gold head, broad pear-shaped white belly and dark back; slate edge shading; orange lower bill edge, dark webbed feet. Actual hulls finish flippers/feet at integer pixels. Standing/swimming body templates separately follow rigid projected volumes. No image generation, downloaded models or old animal masters.

Primary grounding (text read, no claim of opening research figures/photos): [BAS penguins](https://legacy.bas.ac.uk/about_antarctica/wildlife/birds/penguins/index.php), [BAS identification leaflet](https://www.bas.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/public_information_leaflets_antarctic_penguins.pdf), [Griffin and Kram 2000 emperor waddle](https://www.nature.com/articles/35050167), [Clark and Bemis 1979 seven-species swimming](https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/bitstream/handle/2027.42/73876/j.1469-7998.1979.tb03424.x.pdf?sequence=1), [JEB2021 gentoo flipper kinematics](https://doi.org/10.1242/jeb.242140). Gentoo measurements inform articulation, not a claim of exact emperor timing/forces. Species checklist row248 was textual anatomy guidance only.

Limitations: profile22 vs26 proposal; tiny toe/eye/bill clusters, mirrored light and narrow profile overlap; idealized upright weight transfer without body roll/force simulation; understated profile stretch; abrupt land/water/stop transitions; swimming head counter-pitch is deliberate stylization; no water entry, wake, refraction, dive, ice slide or vocalization. See public review-observations.md for actual rejected iterations and inspection evidence.
