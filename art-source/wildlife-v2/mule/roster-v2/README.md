# Fresh mule prototype

Separate roster-v2 identity under tactical 140. Independent species geometry
starts in an empty Blender scene using the pinned grazers-140 family. No old
animal pixels or source are read. The 64px cell, (32,48) anchor and shared
10px/world-unit camera retain the common native scale. Anatomy/proportions,
coat, ears, mane/fleece, feet and tail are owned by the species profile.

The 32-pose walk matches its runtime speed. Torso heave/pitch carries buried
shoulders/hips above independent fixed-length articulated legs and planted
hooves. A separate two-link neck carries the head through the eight-pose graze,
with dense source keys and a separately moving jaw. Camelids use paired toe
meshes; donkey/mule retain full hooves and dark mane/brush. Long-neck proportions
and donkey coat contrast were refined in native/enlarged review before freezing.

Independent audit checks 129 walk and 65 action samples/facing, 6,272 limb links,
784 neck links, 1,176 tail links, 1,244 contacts and 688 planted travel pairs.
Head/neck/body attachments, native jaw angle changes, at least 3px grazing head
travel, fixed local geometry/scales and exact loops are checked from the saved
scene. Palette paint follows actual source parts. Native foot hull compression
retains the exact output silhouette while keeping full geometry evidence in
Blender. Replay must reproduce all outputs byte-for-byte; chronological full
Chromium checks every native/enlarged pose. Exact final art awaits human review.
