# Giant manta draft-v1

Fresh Mobula birostris prototype; no old wildlife or other animal geometry/master was used. Blender 5.2.2 isolated CLI source retains 24 full meshes and repeatable builder. Palette-letter masters are deliberately authored from independently projected hulls, articulated landmarks and depth; integer Pillow finishing does not quantize a render into the final art.

Camera contract: unchanged wildlife-v2 camera, orthographic 40 degrees ABOVE GROUND, yaws down180/up0/left100/right260, 10 pixels/unit. Canvas144 square, anchor72,90, ortho14.4, shift18/144. Idle heights down33/up32/left41/right41 versus roster profile proposal24. The adult's 6.3-unit span is retained rather than shrinking the horizontally broad disc to meet a vertical-height proposal.

Rigid fixed disc/head, diamond pectoral fins, terminal mouth, lateral eyes, white dorsal shoulder patches, dorsal fin, narrow trailing tail and paired cephalic lobes. Two 1.40-unit wing panels per side bend with base25*sin(phase) and distal base+25*sin(phase-pi/2): computed base height apex3, tip apex4 in the twelve-pose cycle. No body bob or translated still is locomotion. Six cephalic links .24/.20/.15 and five .4-unit tail links retain length. Eight action poses unroll the cephalic lobes into a feeding funnel; this is not a barrel roll or gape. Idle holds a slightly down-curved glide. Root fixed; separate preview travel .70 unit/cycle.

84 poses: idle1/swim12/action8 across four facings. All actual meshes stay submerged at Z.339627..3.060373 beneath plane4.3. Ground foot contacts are empty. Source audit reloads the .blend in a fresh Blender process, hashes geometry, checks unit object scale, 1260 articulated links, rigid head and actual projected vertices to 5e-6. Compact pose guides omit repeated full mesh geometry. Output audit checks deterministic replay25 files, alpha/padding/shared palette, all84 silhouettes8-connected,4263 stable full-head pixels, five decoded GIF durations and1340 actual bundled full-Chromium sprite readbacks. These establish integrity, not art quality.

References read as primary text: [NOAA giant manta](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/giant-manta-ray), [NOAA identification framework](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/s3/2023-09/Giant-Manta-Ray-Section-7-Framework.pdf), [Fish et al.2018 oscillatory swimming](https://doi.org/10.1242/jeb.166041), [Fish et al.2016 fin kinematics](https://www.mdpi.com/2226-4310/3/3/20). Remote figures/photos were not visually inspected. Independent local images and actual browser cycles were inspected; see output review-observations.md.

Repeat with workspace Python and Blender CLI --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code1 --python build.py; audit-source.py requires loading manta-ray.blend first. Run author-masters.py, finish.py, capture.mjs, audit-output.py and review-panels.py. BLENDER_BIN and bundled Chromium paths are session tooling, not global configuration. Registered exact bytes are immutable.

Limitations: two-joint wings/angular extremes, tiny eyes/lobes/tail, modest feeding action, mirrored light and discrete idle transition; decorative water without refraction/wake/food simulation. No full roll or water entry. Production draft judgment only; coordinator/human review pending.
