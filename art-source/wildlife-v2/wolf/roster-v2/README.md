# Fresh wolf ground hunter

Tactical 140 authorizes this separate roster-v2 identity. Empty-scene anatomy:
Broad grey chest/ruff, pointed ears, pale muzzle and bushy low tail.
Four constant-length links per limb connect buried shoulder/hip points to
independent planted paws. A three-link tail, separate head and articulated jaw
have their own source motion. No historical animal source or pixels are read.

This source wrapper uses the pinned ground-hunters-140 family mechanics,
finishing and independent audit, with species-specific anatomy/palette/stride
parameters. The shared 10px/world-unit camera uses a 64px cell and (32,48) anchor.
Thirty-two walking and 32 brisk chase poses have independent travel lengths
matched to the corresponding runtime speeds. Eight-pose feeding and display
loops retain all four grounded feet. Torso heave/pitch and head counter-motion
carry the buried limb roots while foot contacts stay independent.

Independent audit samples 129 points per walk/chase/facing and 33 per action,
checking 10,496 limb links, 1,968 tail links, 1,976 ground contacts and 1,376
planted travel pairs. All local meshes/scales remain constant; every clip loops
exactly. Rest-only coat/face/jaw volume guides become separate palette masters;
limbs, paws and tail follow their actual projected source joints. These are
stylized game gaits, not measured anatomical reconstructions.

Native/enlarged inspection, deterministic source replay, two-scale Chromium
playback and shared hunting/game checks are required before registration.
The shared family inputs become immutable with the receipts. Exact final
pixels remain pending human art review; no approval is inferred from inspection.
