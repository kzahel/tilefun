# Fresh buff hen family prototype

Tactical 140 authorizes this fresh roster-v2 scene, superseding the historical
blocked chicken draft without reading its old source or pixels. build.py begins
with an empty Blender scene. Independently editable pear torso, breast/neck,
small skull, short pointed beak, three red comb lobes, paired wattles, folded
wings and raised fan feathers establish hen anatomy. Two articulated legs
have fixed-length femur/tibia/tarsus/toe links and actual three-forward/one-back
toe mesh components. Buried hips follow torso weight transfer.

Native cell 48px, (24,35) ground anchor, unchanged shared camera. The 32-pose
1.28s walk matches 10px/s and keeps one or two planted supports; its phase
closes exactly. Peck uses downward skull rotation and a short connected
head/neck gather. Idle/action retain 100ms poses. Source timing is 100fps,
four source frames per walk pose and ten per action pose.

Species-specific feather and face palette-letter masters are authored from
rest geometry guides once, then move rigidly through the evaluated body/head
bases. Actual separate joints/toe axes drive feet. Saved-source auditing
checks 65 half-poses/facing, constant geometry, 1,056 fixed links, planted
travel and loop closure. Pixel finishing requires palette/binary alpha,
padding and visible native torso motion; replay and full Chromium capture
check the exact outputs. Final pixels await human review. This prototype
provides a ground-bird source/motion reference, not a human approval.
