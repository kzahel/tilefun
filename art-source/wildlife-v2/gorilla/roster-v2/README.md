# Fresh gorilla prototype

Tactical 140 authorizes this separate roster-v2 identity. The builder starts
from an empty Blender scene and reads no historical gorilla pixels or source.
A deep barrel torso, wide shoulders, silver saddle, narrow pelvis, heavy brow,
flat nose and large articulated forearms distinguish the primate silhouette.
Four fixed-length links per limb connect buried shoulders/hips to planted
knuckles or hind soles. Individual knuckle bosses carry the forelimb support.

Native 96px cell, (48,72) anchor, shared 10px/world-unit camera. The diagonal
32-pose walk lasts 2.24s and travels 22.4px at the runtime's 10px/s. A .76
stance fraction maintains three or four supports. The torso heaves, pitches
and sways above stationary contacts; the head counters the body pitch.
The 16-pose, 800ms chest beat lifts and alternates the forearms while both
hind feet stay planted. Four source substeps per action pose prevent long
arms cutting across their joints between exported samples.

The broad gait choices are informed by the primary research paper
[Gorilla knuckle-walking mechanics suggest independent evolution in African
great apes](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-21780-2).
Timing, proportions and action are authored game approximations, not measured
motion reconstruction. Rest-only volume guides support separate body/head
palette paint; actual joint and hand geometry drives the articulated parts.

The independent saved-scene audit checks 65 walk half-poses/facing and 129
chest-beat samples/facing, fixed link lengths, buried limb roots, sole contact,
planted travel, constant local geometry and scale, and exact loop closure.
Final source replay and two-scale Chromium playback verify committed output.
All exact final pixels remain pending human review; agent inspection does
not create an approval. Ordinary builds consume the committed bank.
