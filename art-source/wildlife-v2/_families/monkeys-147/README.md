# Ground primates, continuation147

Five distinct editable sources: capuchin cap and bare face; lemur pointed muzzle,
eye mask and articulated ringed tail; mandrill blue facial ridges, red nose and
short tail; proboscis pendulous nose and pale belly; macaque shaggy cheeks, bare
pink face and short tail. Ground walking, foraging and startle escape use shared
Fauna authority. Tree climbing and brachiation are future work.

Rigid mesh volumes, buried limb roots, fixed four-link arms/legs and three-link
tails; planted contacts and torso weight transfer are independently audited.
