# Forest herbivores, autonomous 140

Fresh beaver, capybara, koala, wombat, quokka and Malayan tapir scenes retain
moving bodies and four fixed-link planted limbs. Beaver uses a flattened
three-link paddle tail and incisors; capybara blunt muzzle and tiny ears;
koala broad nose and large furry ears; wombat broad haunches/short tail; quokka
rounded ears and a narrow tail; tapir black-and-cream saddle and a short snout.
These are ground walking, grazing/foraging and escape profiles. They do not add
swimming, climbing, tree cutting or pouch simulation. Exact pixels await human
review after saved-scene audits, byte-identical replay and browser checks.
