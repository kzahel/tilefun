# Shelled animals and amphibians, autonomous 140

Fresh salamander, newt, axolotl, frilled-neck lizard, land tortoise and pond turtle
scenes retain low moving bodies and fixed planted limbs. Closed shell/scute,
frill, external gill and crest geometry distinguish their anatomy. These are
bounded ground/bottom-walking forage/escape profiles: land animals remain dry,
newt uses its terrestrial phase; axolotl and pond turtle stay in compatible water. They do not add an
amphibious transition or a new swimming controller. Exact pixels await review.
