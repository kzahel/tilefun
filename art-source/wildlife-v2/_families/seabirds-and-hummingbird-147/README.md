# Seabirds and hummingbird, continuation147

Original pelican with long bill/gular pouch and broad webbed feet; blue-footed
booby with white body/brown wings/blue feet; male great frigatebird with long
pointed wings, forked tail and red throat pouch; ruby-throated hummingbird with
slender bill, green coat/red gorget and faster wing strokes. Two linked tucking
legs and fixed shoulder/elbow feather wings attach to closed editable volumes.

Shared Fauna authority supplies bounded landing-to-landing flights. Swimming,
open-air soaring and continuous hovering are future work. No animated scaling or
pixel recoloring of existing banks. Exact final art stays pending human review.

Larger birds retain a continuous hock plane derived from the hip/elbow geometry.
Quarter-pose source keys and independent eighth-pose checks keep joined legs
through tucking; the audit samples 257 flight poses per facing.
