# Distinct fish, continuation147

New editable ocean sunfish, swordfish, pufferfish and anglerfish. Each uses its
own body/head proportions, fin shapes and caudal anatomy. Sunfish dorsal/anal
fins and clavus, swordfish bill and forked tail, puffer spines/paired fins and
angler lure/toothed jaw remain rigid-volume source geometry. No animated scale.
Saved-scene tail lengths, fin attachment, body motion and closure are audited.
Exact final art stays pending human review. Original marine helpers stay frozen.
