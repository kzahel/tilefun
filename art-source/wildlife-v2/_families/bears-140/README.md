# Bear sources, tactical 140

Fresh empty-scene bear anatomy uses broad shoulders/haunches, rounded ears,
separate skull/muzzle/jaw, large flat paws and short articulated tails. Panda
has black shoulders/limbs/ears/eye patches and a white body/face. Polar bear has
an elongated white body/neck/muzzle and contrasting nose/eyes. These are
stylized game animals, not anatomical measurements or ecological claims.

Four constant-length links per limb, independent flat soles, moving torso and
head counter-motion retain planted travel. Walk and chase have 32 poses each;
feeding/foraging and warning display have eight each. Saved-scene audits sample
129 points per gait and 33 per action/facing, verifying meshes/scales, links,
contacts, roots, world travel and loops independently. Panda's jaw carries a
modeled bamboo stem/leaves, visible only during the feeding clip through keyed
render/viewport visibility; the independent audit checks both attachment and
visibility. Public Panda metadata names that clip action for shared fauna AI.

Registered inputs become immutable. Native/enlarged inspection, exact source
replay, Chromium playback and game checks never approve final pixels. Every new
identity remains pending human art review. Ordinary builds use committed assets;
WILDLIFE_OUTPUT_DIR isolates unfinished previews.
