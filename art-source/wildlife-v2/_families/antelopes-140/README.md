# Horned grazer family, tactical 140

Empty-scene antelope sources share fixed-length articulated neck/limb mechanics.
Independent proportions, skulls, horns, ears, muzzle and coats distinguish six
animals. Gemsbok has long straight horns and face/flank masks; gazelle shorter
lyre horns; impala tall swept lyre horns and dark rump marks; springbok short
hooked horns and flank stripe; saiga a modeled swollen hanging nose and small
horns; gerenuk a tall articulated neck and slim body. Horns are tapered closed
mesh frusta parented to the skull. These are stylized game animals.

Two constant neck links carry the skull through grazing; four fixed links per
limb connect buried roots to planted independent cloven hooves. Torso heave and
pitch do not move foot contacts. Dense action keys preserve jaw chewing and
neck attachment. Native coat/face/jaw paint follows the actual projected source
parts; near flank markings use their projected surface guides after rib-cage
centroid sorting. Independent audit reads only the saved scene and camera.

Registered inputs are immutable. Source and pixel inspection, byte-identical
replay, two-scale playback and shared game checks do not approve final art.
All exact new identities remain pending human review. Ordinary builds use
committed assets; WILDLIFE_OUTPUT_DIR isolates drafts.
