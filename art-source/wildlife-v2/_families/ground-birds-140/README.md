# Ground birds, autonomous run 140

Fresh ostrich, emu, cassowary, kiwi, kakapo, turkey and peacock scenes use two
four-link planted legs, moving rigid bodies, two fixed neck links, articulated
heads/beaks, attached rotating wings and fixed-length feather volumes.
Ostrich feet have two toes; emu/cassowary three. Kiwi, turkey and peacock have
three forward toes plus a rear toe; kakapo has two forward and two rear toes.
This source batch supplies ground locomotion, foraging/display and escape.
It does not add an airborne flight controller for turkey, peacock or kakapo.

Cassowary casque/blue neck/red wattles, kiwi long bill, kakapo facial disc,
ostrich white wings/skin neck, emu shaggy coat, turkey wattle/banded fan and
peacock crest/eyespots/folding train are distinct editable source geometry.
The feather fans spread by rotating fixed volumes about an attached root;
they do not scale or warp painted frames. All profiles retain the shared
four-facing camera and ten native pixels per world unit.

Independent saved-scene audits sample eighth poses between quarter-pose keys
and check limb/neck endpoints, planted
travel, toes, buried hips, feather and wing roots, fixed local mesh coordinates,
scales and loop closure. Native finishing paints actual posed mesh projections.
Exact banks and their input helpers become immutable on registration. Mechanical
and browser checks do not confer human approval of the resulting pixels.
