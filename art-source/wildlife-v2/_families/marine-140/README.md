# Marine animals, autonomous run 140

Fresh whale shark, dolphin, narwhal, humpback, blue whale and sea turtle scenes
use fixed-volume heaving/pitching bodies, articulated jaws, three fixed tail
links and attached rotating flippers. The shark uses a lateral vertical tail;
cetaceans use vertical strokes of horizontal flukes. Turtle has four flippers
and a short tail. Shell scutes, whale-shark spots/gills/flat mouth, dolphin
rostrum/dorsal fin, narwhal tapered spiral tusk, humpback tubercles/long white
flippers and blue-whale proportions/pleats are distinct editable anatomy.

Dolphin/narwhal have independent swim, feed, display and pursuit clips. The
four neutral species have idle, swim and foraging clips. Filter feeding is a
visual action, without a simulated plankton resource. Large neutral whales use
128/160px cells and 41 columns, retaining the fixed ten-pixels-per-world-unit camera
while staying below the supported 8192px texture dimension.

The independent audit reopens saved scenes and checks quarter poses, fixed
mesh coordinates/scales, tail endpoints, flipper roots, jaw motion, body heave
and loop closure. Pixel finishing paints actual posed source volumes and
countershaded surface faces. Exact banks and helpers freeze on registration;
mechanical checks do not confer human art approval.
