# Heavy bovid sources, tactical 140

Fresh empty-scene anatomy shares fixed limb/neck mechanics, with independent
bodies, skulls, swept tapered horns, lateral ears, hooves and coats. Bison has
high modeled shoulders, shaggy front quarters, forehead and beard; buffalo a
broad grey barrel and long crescent horns; musk ox a deep coat skirt, saddle,
horn boss and downward/upward curled horns; wildebeest high front quarters,
lighter haunches, dark mane/forehead/beard and outward/upward horns.

All horn segments are closed tapered mesh frusta parented to the skull. Four
fixed limb links connect buried roots to independently planted cloven hooves;
two fixed neck links carry grazing skull/jaw motion; three tail links follow
the pelvis. Torso heave/pitch and head counter-motion preserve contact soles.
Native paint is reprojected from actual rigid body/head/jaw transforms. The
saved-scene audit independently checks constant geometry/scales, connected
limbs/necks/tails, planted travel, native motion, attachment and exact loops.
These are stylized game animals, not measured anatomical reconstructions.

Registered inputs are immutable. Ordinary builds use committed assets, and
WILDLIFE_OUTPUT_DIR isolates drafts. Agent native/source/replay/game checks
never approve final art: every exact new identity remains pending human review.
