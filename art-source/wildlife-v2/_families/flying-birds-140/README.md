# Short-flight birds, autonomous 140

Fresh pigeon, dove, sparrow, parrot, toucan and great hornbill use distinct bills,
coats and closed feather volumes. Two fixed three-link legs rest on the ground
and tuck during physical short flights. Shoulder and elbow hinges carry two
rigid wing sections and overlapping flight feathers; each hinge is audited
against its own parent. Delivery and physical push/landing clocks match.
These are bounded landing-to-landing flights using existing land navigation;
no unrestricted aerial navigation, perching or airborne hunting is claimed.
Explicit food-web prey and natural-only replacement policies apply on landing.
Exact final pixels await human review after source/replay/browser checks.
