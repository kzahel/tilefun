# Ground specialists, continuation147

Meerkat, giant anteater, aardvark, armadillo and pangolin have distinct fixed-volume
anatomy, ground walks, body weight transfer and foraging. Anteater tubular muzzle,
contrasting shoulder stripe and deep bushy tail; aardvark upright ears/long snout;
armadillo closed upper armor bands; pangolin overlapping closed scales on body and
articulated tail; meerkat slender body, eye patches and tapered tail. No curling,
burrowing or sentinel rearing is implemented by this ground gait.

Buried four-link limb roots, planted contacts, tail links and every closed armor
volume are independently audited. Original sources stay immutable. Exact new art
stays pending human review.
