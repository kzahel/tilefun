This source-only scooter keeps the immutable Explorer upright GLB and profile separate. It is a new rounded legshield, step-through floor and rear cowling template. The editable component master uses runtime topology; it is not a high-to-low transfer claim. Parent shared two-wheel browser qualification remains pending.

The constructor accepts `--config PATH --variant-id ID --scratch` after Blender's `--`. The ID must equal the explicit configuration ID. The profile bytes are checked before construction. Its five world-pixel contacts are divided by `exportWorldScale` and translated by the single `riderMountSource`; the avatar is never rescaled or re-posed. Saddle top, grip axes and continuous floor top derive from those contacts. Both soles must have equal Z for this planar floor template.

For another measured fit, author an owned new family/config and profile/rider pins, one mount, saddle dimensions, grip radius/length/headstock, floor extent and rails. Adjust mechanical cowling/shield/fork dimensions only in the owned new template, then rerun all contacts, bounded semantic pairs, wheel cylinders, crown region, floor rays and native views. These parameters do not establish universal avatar reach or automatic bike fit. Fixed body geometry coordinates are mechanical family anatomy, not hidden identity offsets. The immutable Explorer pose remains straddled; no knees-together scooter pose is claimed.

The rider-neutral constructor needs exactly four own inputs (`build.py`, `geometry.py`, `family.json`, `raw-audit.py`) and two read-only shared inputs (the profile and `catalog-cycle.py`).
